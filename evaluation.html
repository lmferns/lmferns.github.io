What the data changed

At decision 1.07, 8 of 24 technicians started the repair once their lock was on, without testing for voltage. The procedure screen before it covered the five lockout steps, but never said that a lock does not remove stored energy.

The procedure screen was revised to name stored energy and capacitors explicitly. In a second sample cohort of 20, unsafe first-attempt choices at 1.07 fell from 8 of 24 (33%) to 2 of 20 (10%).
