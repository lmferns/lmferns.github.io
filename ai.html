AI-Enabled Instructional Design

Faster first drafts. Human judgment built in.

I treat AI as a structured content transformer, not a replacement for instructional judgment. The workflows below pair prompt engineering and light automation with a human quality check, so the repetitive part of the work gets faster while the accuracy still gets a second set of eyes before anything ships.

01

HSA vs. FSA: Making Smart Choices for Your Health Savings

  • Rise 360
  • Articulate AI Assistant
  • CRIT prompt framework
Under 2 minutesfor a complete, scenario-based, four-lesson course

The problem

Employees heading into open enrollment often don't understand the real difference between a Health Savings Account (HSA) and a Flexible Spending Account (FSA), and building a full, scenario-based course to teach that distinction from scratch is typically a multi-day effort.

What I built

I wrote the prompt using the CRIT framework (Context, Role, Interview, Task), developed by Geoff Woods, author of The AI-Driven Leader, giving Articulate's AI Assistant the background and a defined role, then having it interview me for clarifying details before generating. In under two minutes, that single prompt produced a complete four-lesson course, "HSA vs. FSA: Making Smart Choices for Your Health Savings," including scenario-based narratives, flashcard, sorting, accordion, and tab interactions, embedded knowledge checks throughout, a final quiz mixing multiple choice, multiple response, and fill-in-the-blank questions, and nine custom AI-generated images, all without leaving Rise 360. I then reviewed and refined that first draft, since AI speeds up the build but doesn't replace instructional judgment.

Impact

A full, exam-ready, scenario-based benefits course built in under two minutes, start to finish, showing how a well-structured prompt turns Articulate's AI Assistant into a fast, dependable first-draft tool, with a human review step always built in.

02

AI-Enabled Storyline Import

  • Microsoft Copilot
  • Articulate Storyline
  • Excel import
30+ min → secondsper exam setup, now a standard team workflow

The problem

Rebuilding assessments from a subject matter expert's Word document into Articulate Storyline's import format was slow and error prone. Copying and pasting, re-keying answers, and reformatting exam content that arrived differently every time.

What I built

A structured Copilot prompt that treats the Word document as the single source of truth, with strict rules that block AI tendencies like rewriting or inventing content, and a locked output schema that matches Storyline's Excel import format exactly. It handles messy, inconsistently formatted source docs (unlabeled correct answers, mixed question types, extra instructional text) and still returns clean, import ready rows.

The safeguard

Automation handles the transformation, and a short human quality check (spot checking question types, asterisk placement, and one row per question) still catches anything that needs a second look before import.

Impact

Cut typical exam setup from 30 or more minutes down to seconds, and the process was adopted as a standard workflow on my team. Note: the screenshots below use fictional sample content in place of the original client materials.

03

AI-Enabled Storyline Notes Clean Up

  • PowerPoint VBA macro
  • Articulate Storyline
  • Storyboard import
Copy → Run → Importa three-step cleanup that keeps the original storyboard intact

The problem

Storyboards built in PowerPoint carry two audiences worth of content in the same file: developer notes and interaction instructions for the build team, and the narration that should reach learners. Once a storyboard gets imported into Storyline, that mix comes through as messy, unusable notes: stray direction like [SHOW BOX 1] and [NARRATION] tags, inconsistent formatting, and placeholder text that all needs cleaning up by hand before anyone can use it.

What I built

A VBA macro, paired with a clear set of cleanup rules, that generates a second, import ready copy of the storyboard. It strips out development only direction and standardizes the notes formatting, while leaving the original storyboard untouched as the source of truth for interaction direction and reviewer comments.

Impact

Faster Storyline builds, cleaner learner-facing notes, less manual editing, and consistent formatting across every storyboard that goes through the process. Note: the screenshots below use fictional sample content in place of the original client materials.

04

AI-Accelerated Storyboard Design

  • AI storyboarding
  • Articulate Storyline
  • Merrill's First Principles
  • Gagné's Nine Events
2 days, not 2 weeksfrom legacy content to a build-ready, reviewable prototype

The problem

A safety-compliance course for field technicians had gone stagnant, with eight lessons of linear, click-to-expand reading, capped at the lowest level of Bloom's taxonomy despite covering hazards with real consequences. Leadership needed a redesigned, interactive prototype fast, to get in front of subject matter experts for review, not a slow multi-week storyboarding cycle.

What I built

I used AI to rapidly translate the legacy content into a full, build-ready storyboard, a first-pass redesign in 2 days instead of the typical two-week cycle, then layered in Merrill's First Principles (task-centered instruction) nested within Gagné's Nine Events at the lesson level. Passive "read the topic" screens became one continuous scenario following a single technician through a realistic day of decisions, with every module closing on a branching, consequence-based choice: an incorrect answer plays out a realistic consequence before looping back to retry, instead of a simple right/wrong marker. Objectives moved from Remember/Identify to Apply/Analyze, with retrieval distributed across modules plus a cumulative, interleaved capstone simulation mixing every hazard type together.

Impact

A build-ready, reviewable prototype in 2 days instead of two weeks, giving SMEs something concrete to react to while the direction was still easy to change, plus a repeatable pattern for converting other legacy, passive courses into branching Storyline builds.
