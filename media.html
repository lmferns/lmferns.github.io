01

SME Video Optimization (Raw vs. Polished)

  • Panopto
  • Articulate Replay
  • Video editing

The challenge

SMEs often send raw screen recordings full of dead air, glitches and confusing clicks. I took a 2:25 unedited screencast and turned it into a clean tutorial that keeps learners focused on the steps that matter.

What I did

  • Cut the "ums," long loading pauses and stray mouse movements in Panopto and Articulate Replay.
  • Added callouts and overlays in Replay to point learners to fields and buttons that were easy to miss.
  • Balanced the audio and synced the narration with the on-screen actions.
  • Used Panopto for recording and Replay for the instructional polish, then branded the final video.

Result

  • A shorter video with nothing important lost.
  • Raw SME footage brought up to our corporate training standards.
  • Learners can follow the software steps without distractions.
02

Career Services Microlearning Series

  • Scripting
  • Video production
  • Microlearning

The challenge

Students in the middle of a job search need quick help with specific tasks like networking or resumes, and they often don't have time for a full workshop. I turned career development topics into short videos they could use when they needed them.

What I did

  • Wrote and produced scripts like "The 30-Second Commercial," turning career advice into clear steps.
  • Built that module around a 6-step framework, from introducing yourself to research-based talking points.
  • Showed non-verbal skills (eye contact, handshake, tone) on screen, not just in the narration.
  • Published the videos as standalone modules on the URI Career Center (CCEE) website.

Result

  • Students get help at the moment they need it.
  • They can come back to the videos throughout school and after graduation.
  • The library covers networking, resume writing and cover letters, and became a main onboarding resource for URI students.
03

Networking & Branding (Powtoon)

  • Powtoon
  • Voiceover
  • Audio editing
2,400+ viewersreached with in-house narration and animation

The challenge

The URI Center for Career and Experiential Education needed short videos on the unwritten rules of the job search. The topics change often, so we needed to produce and update the videos in-house, without paying for outside voice talent.

What I did

  • Wrote and recorded the narration myself, in a warm, encouraging tone for college students.
  • Edited the audio in Panopto and Articulate Replay and timed it to the Powtoon animation.
  • Built the script in three parts (the Who, the How and the Brand) to fit everything into under two minutes.
  • Took each video from recording to final MP4, ready for the URI website and YouTube.

Result

  • No outside vendor costs for audio.
  • The video has reached more than 2,400 viewers.
  • Updates are quick: I can re-record a line instead of redoing the whole video.

04

Infographics & Visual Job Aids

  • Canva
  • Miro
  • Lucidchart
  • Snagit

The challenge

Learners were making process errors and using the wrong forms because the documentation was too dense. They needed quick visual references they could check while they worked.

What I did

  • Mapped the flow of information in Miro and Lucidchart first.
  • Designed branded infographics in Canva, using visual hierarchy to make the critical compliance steps stand out.
  • Reviewed drafts with leadership and SMEs and revised them for accuracy and clarity.
  • Formatted each piece to display well in the LMS and internal channels.

Result

  • Process accuracy and form completion improved after rollout.
  • Download numbers and feedback showed people were using them, and shaped the next versions.
  • The format became a template for future job aids.
